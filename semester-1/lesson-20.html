Why This Matters

You have built the foundation of your project. Now it is time to step back, review what you have accomplished, present your milestone to the class, and set goals for Semester 2.

What You Will Learn

  • Review Semester 1 progress.
  • Present your milestone.
  • Set goals for Semester 2.

Words to Know

milestone review presentation feedback goal reflection

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What Have You Built? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What have you accomplished this semester?

  • Problem statement
  • Plan
  • Design
  • Prototype

Today you present your milestone.

Part 2 — Milestone Presentation Structure 10–30 min

Follow this structure:

  1. Problem statement and user needs.
  2. Decomposition and models.
  3. Requirements and architecture.
  4. Prototype demonstration.
  5. Challenges and next steps.

Part 3 — Preparation 30–55 min

Prepare your 5-minute presentation.

Part 4 — Presentations 55–85 min

Each group presents for 5 minutes.

Class asks questions.

Teacher gives feedback.

Part 5 — Feedback and Goal Setting 85–95 min

Reflect on feedback.

Set 3 goals for Semester 2.

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

What is your top goal for Semester 2?
