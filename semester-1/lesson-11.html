Roles, Responsibilities, and Teamwork

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 3 · Project Planning & Management Team Roles, Collaboration

Why This Matters

A project is built by a team, not one person. When everyone knows their role and respects each other, the work flows smoothly. In this lesson, you will define roles, create a team charter, and practise effective collaboration.

What You Will Learn

  • Define team roles for a project.
  • Create a team charter.
  • Practise effective collaboration.

Words to Know

role responsibility collaboration team charter accountability communication conflict resolution

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What Makes a Good Team? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What makes a team work well?

  • Clear roles
  • Communication
  • Respect
  • Shared goals

Projects succeed when teams are organized.

Part 2 — Team Roles 10–30 min

Common roles in a project team:

  • Project lead: keeps the team on track.
  • Developer: writes code or builds the system.
  • Designer: creates the interface and visuals.
  • Data manager: handles data collection and database.
  • Tester: tests and reports issues.
  • Presenter: prepares and delivers the presentation.

Roles can overlap, but everyone must be accountable.

Part 3 — Assign Roles Together 30–55 min

Use the library system project as an example:

  • Assign roles and responsibilities.
  • Create a team charter:
    • Team name.
    • Roles.
    • Meeting schedule.
    • Communication rules.
    • Conflict resolution plan.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Create Your Team Charter 55–85 min

Create your charter:

  • Team name and roles.
  • Weekly meeting schedule.
  • Communication channel.
  • How decisions are made.
  • How conflicts are resolved.
  • How progress is tracked.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Present your team charter.

Discuss: What will you do if a team member does not contribute?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

What is your role, and what are your top 3 responsibilities?

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can define roles and create a team charter.

Support: Use a team charter template.

Challenge: Add a peer evaluation plan.

Before Next Lesson

Finalize your team charter and share with your team.
