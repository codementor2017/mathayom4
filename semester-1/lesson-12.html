Risk Assessment and Documentation

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 3 · Project Planning & Management Risk Register, Documentation

Why This Matters

Even the best plans can go wrong. A sensor might break. A team member might get sick. The internet might go down. A risk register helps you prepare for problems before they happen, and good documentation keeps your project organized.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify risks in a project.
  • Create a risk register.
  • Document project progress.

Words to Know

risk risk register likelihood impact mitigation documentation log

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What Could Go Wrong? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What could go wrong in your project?

  • Sensor breaks.
  • Data is missing.
  • Team member is sick.
  • Code bug.
  • Time runs out.

Risk assessment prepares you for problems.

Part 2 — Risk Register 10–30 min

Learn the risk register format:

  • Risk description.
  • Likelihood (1–5).
  • Impact (1–5).
  • Priority = likelihood × impact.
  • Mitigation plan.
  • Owner.

Example:

  • Sensor breaks — Likelihood 3, Impact 4, Priority 12 — Order backup sensor.
  • Team member sick — Likelihood 2, Impact 3, Priority 6 — Cross-train roles.
  • Data missing — Likelihood 2, Impact 4, Priority 8 — Backup data daily.

Documentation includes:

  • Weekly progress log.
  • Meeting notes.
  • Version history.
  • Change log.

Part 3 — Create a Risk Register Together 30–55 min

Create a risk register for the library system.

Include at least 5 risks.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Risk Register and Documentation Plan 55–85 min

Create:

  • Risk register with at least 5 risks.
  • Documentation plan: what to log, when, and by whom.
  • Sample weekly progress log.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Present your risk register.

Discuss: What is your highest-priority risk?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

Name one risk and its mitigation plan.

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can identify risks and create a mitigation plan.

Support: Use a risk register template.

Challenge: Create a contingency budget.
