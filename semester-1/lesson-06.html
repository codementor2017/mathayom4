Semester 1 · Lesson 6

Conducting User Needs Assessment

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 2 · Problem Identification & Scoping Surveys, Interviews, Empathy

Why This Matters

A project only succeeds if it meets real user needs. But you cannot guess what users need — you have to ask them, watch them, and listen with empathy. In this lesson, you will learn how to conduct surveys and interviews, and how to create personas that represent your users.

What You Will Learn

  • Design a user needs assessment.
  • Conduct surveys or interviews.
  • Analyse user needs data.

Words to Know

user needs survey interview empathy stakeholder persona requirement

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Who Is Your User? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: Who will use your project?

Users could be students, teachers, farmers, patients, shoppers, or librarians.

Projects succeed when they meet real user needs.

Part 2 — User Needs Assessment 10–30 min

There are three main methods:

  • Survey — many users, quick answers.
  • Interview — one user, deep answers.
  • Observation — watch users in action.

Use the empathy mindset:

  • Put yourself in the user's situation.
  • Ask "what do you need?" not "what do you want?"
  • Listen more than you talk.

Create a persona — a fictional but realistic user:

  • Name, age, role
  • Goals
  • Frustrations
  • Needs

Part 3 — Practise Together: Library System 30–55 min

Let's design a user assessment for the library system:

  • Create 5 survey questions.
  • Create 3 interview questions.
  • Create one persona.

Write on the board together.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Conduct User Assessment 55–85 min

In your group:

  • Create your survey or interview guide.
  • Conduct at least 5 surveys or 2 interviews.
  • Create 2 personas.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your personas.

Discuss: What surprised you about your users' needs?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

Write one user need and how your project will address it.

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can conduct a user assessment and create personas.

Support: Use a survey template and persona template.

Challenge: Conduct a focus group.

Before Next Lesson
