Semester 1 · Lesson 8

Exploring Domains: Health, Agriculture, Energy, Environment

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 2 · Problem Identification & Scoping Domain Deep Dive

Why This Matters

Technology solves problems in every part of life — from growing food to keeping people healthy to protecting the environment. Understanding the different domains helps you see where your project can make the biggest difference.

What You Will Learn

  • Describe the key challenges in each domain.
  • Identify how computational thinking can address them.
  • Connect your project to a real-world domain.

Words to Know

domain health care agriculture energy environment food trade transaction

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Domains in Daily Life 0–10 min

Where do you see technology solving problems?

  • Health apps
  • Smart farms
  • Solar panels
  • Recycling apps
  • Food delivery

Each domain has unique challenges and opportunities.

Part 2 — Domain Deep Dive 10–30 min

Key challenges and opportunities in each domain:

  • Health: patient tracking, appointment reminders, medication schedules.
  • Agriculture: soil monitoring, automatic watering, pest detection, crop routing.
  • Energy: usage monitoring, solar optimization, smart grids.
  • Environment: air/water quality, waste management, recycling.
  • Food: nutrition tracking, portion control, food waste reduction.
  • Trade/Transactions: ordering, payment, inventory, delivery routing.
  • Library: book search, recommendations, borrowing history.

Your project should connect to one of these domains and solve a real problem.

Part 3 — Match Projects to Domains 30–55 min

Which domain does each project idea belong to?

  • Automatic plant watering → Agriculture.
  • Air quality monitor → Environment.
  • Calorie tracker → Health/Food.
  • Solar panel optimizer → Energy.
  • Library recommendation → Library.
  • Online ordering system → Trade.
  • Patient reminder system → Health.

Discuss your answers.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Domain Analysis 55–85 min

In your group, analyse your domain:

  • Key challenges.
  • Existing solutions.
  • Gaps.
  • How computational thinking can help.
  • How your project fits.

Create a domain poster.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Present your domain analysis.

Discuss: Which domain has the greatest need for innovation?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

Name one challenge in your domain and how your project addresses it.

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can describe domain challenges and connect your project.
