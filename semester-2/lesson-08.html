Accessibility and Error Handling

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 7 · User Interaction & Interface Accessibility, Errors

Why This Matters

Not everyone uses a system the same way. Some users have visual impairments, some have slow internet, and some will make mistakes. Accessibility makes your system usable by everyone. Error handling makes your system graceful when something goes wrong.

What You Will Learn

  • Apply accessibility principles.
  • Add error handling to your program.
  • Test for accessibility and errors.

Words to Know

accessibility error handling try/except alt text contrast keyboard navigation

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Who Uses Your System? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: Can everyone use your system?

  • People with disabilities.
  • People with slow internet.
  • People with older devices.

Accessibility makes your system usable by everyone.

Part 2 — Accessibility and Error Handling 10–30 min

Accessibility features:

  • Alt text for images.
  • High contrast colours.
  • Large, readable fonts.
  • Keyboard navigation.
  • Clear labels.

Error handling in Python:

try:
    result = divide(a, b)
except ZeroDivisionError:
    print("Cannot divide by zero.")

Good systems handle errors gracefully.

Part 3 — Add Accessibility and Errors Together 30–55 min

Add alt text and labels.

Add try/except to your code.

Test with keyboard navigation.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Improve Accessibility and Errors 55–85 min

Add:

  • Alt text to all images.
  • Labels to all forms.
  • Try/except in at least 3 places.

Test with keyboard only.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your accessible system.

Discuss: What accessibility feature was most important?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

List 3 accessibility features and 2 error handling cases.
