Semester 2 · Lesson 20

Final Project Presentation and Reflection

Previous Course Home
100 minutes Phase 10 · Ethics, Impact & Presentation Present, Reflect

Why This Matters

You have spent a whole year building something real. Today is the day you share it with the world. This lesson is about presenting your final project, reflecting on your journey, and celebrating how far you have come.

What You Will Learn

  • Present your final project.
  • Reflect on the project process.
  • Provide peer feedback.

Words to Know

presentation demonstration reflection peer feedback evaluation celebration

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Ready to Present 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What are you most proud of in your project?

Today you present your year-long project.

Part 2 — Presentation Tips 10–30 min

  • Speak clearly and confidently.
  • Show the system in action.
  • Explain the computational thinking behind it.
  • Share user feedback.
  • Reflect on challenges and growth.
  • Answer questions.

Part 3 — Final Preparation 30–50 min

Finalize your presentation.

Test equipment.

Part 4 — Presentations 50–85 min

Each group presents for 10 minutes:

  • Problem and users.
  • Design and architecture.
  • Prototype demonstration.
  • Testing and feedback.
  • Impact assessment.
  • Reflection.

Class asks questions.

Teacher gives feedback using rubric.

Part 5 — Reflection and Peer Feedback 85–95 min

Write a reflection:

  • "What was the hardest part?"
  • "What are you most proud of?"
  • "How did computational thinking help?"
  • "What would you do differently?"

Give peer feedback.

Part 6 — Celebration and Closure 95–100 min

Teacher gives final comments.

Celebrate all projects.

Distribute certificates or awards.

Final Assessment

Summative: Final project rubric.

Success criteria:

  • Complete project with database, interaction, automation.
  • Portfolio with all sections.
  • Clear presentation.
  • Reflection.
  • Peer feedback.

Support: Allow group presentations with individual contributions.

Challenge: Publish your project online.

Final Homework

Write a final reflection on the year-long project.
