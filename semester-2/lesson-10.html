IoT Integration for Real-World Projects

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 8 · Automation & Integration IoT, Connectivity

Why This Matters

IoT (Internet of Things) means connecting sensors to the internet so data can be seen and controlled from anywhere. A farmer can check soil moisture from a phone. A doctor can monitor a patient remotely. In this lesson, you will integrate IoT into your project.

What You Will Learn

  • Explain how IoT connects sensors to the internet.
  • Build a simple IoT prototype or simulation.
  • Connect IoT data to your project.

Words to Know

IoT sensor connectivity cloud dashboard MQTT API real-time

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What is IoT? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What IoT devices do you know?

  • Smart lights.
  • Smartwatch.
  • Smart farm sensors.

IoT connects sensors to the internet.

Part 2 — IoT Architecture 10–30 min

The architecture:

  • Sensor → microcontroller → internet → cloud → app.

Communication protocols:

  • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MQTT, HTTP.

Python example:

import requests

data = {"moisture": 20, "temperature": 30}
requests.post("https://api.example.com/data", json=data)

Part 3 — Build an IoT Simulation Together 30–55 min

Simulate sending sensor data to a cloud dashboard.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Integrate IoT into Your Project 55–85 min

Add IoT to your project:

  • Add IoT sensors (real or simulated).
  • Send data to a dashboard or log.
  • Trigger actions based on data.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your IoT prototype.

Discuss: What IoT data is most useful for your project?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

Describe your IoT data flow in 4 steps.
