Social and Environmental Impact Assessment

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 10 · Ethics, Impact & Presentation Impact Analysis

Why This Matters

Every project has an impact — some positive, some negative. A good project builder thinks carefully about who benefits, who might be harmed, and how to increase the good while reducing the harm. This is called impact assessment.

What You Will Learn

  • Assess the social impact of your project.
  • Assess the environmental impact.
  • Identify ways to increase positive impact.

Words to Know

social impact environmental impact sustainability benefit harm stakeholder

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What is Impact? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What impact does your project have?

  • On users.
  • On the community.
  • On the environment.

Every project has impact — positive and negative.

Part 2 — Impact Assessment 10–30 min

Types of impact:

  • Social: improves lives, saves time, increases access.
  • Environmental: reduces waste, saves energy, protects resources.
  • Economic: saves money, creates value.
  • Ethical: respects privacy, promotes fairness.

How to assess:

  • Identify stakeholders.
  • List benefits.
  • List potential harms.
  • Suggest ways to reduce harm.

Part 3 — Assess a Sample Project Together 30–55 min

Assess the automatic plant control system:

  • Social: saves water, helps farmers.
  • Environmental: reduces water waste.
  • Economic: lowers costs.
  • Ethical: fair access to technology.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Assess Your Project 55–85 min

Assess your project:

  • Stakeholders.
  • Benefits.
  • Harms.
  • Ways to reduce harm.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Present your impact report.

Discuss: How can you increase your positive impact?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

List one positive and one negative impact of your project.

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can assess social and environmental impact.
