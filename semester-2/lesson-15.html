Collecting and Analyzing User Feedback

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 9 · Testing & Iteration Feedback Analysis

Why This Matters

User feedback is a gift. It tells you exactly what to improve. But feedback can be messy — you need to organize it, find patterns, and prioritize what to fix first. That is what this lesson is about.

What You Will Learn

  • Collect user feedback.
  • Analyse feedback to identify patterns.
  • Prioritize improvements.

Words to Know

feedback survey interview analysis pattern priority improvement

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Why Feedback Matters 0–10 min

Ask yourself: Why do we need user feedback?

  • To improve.
  • To fix issues.
  • To meet user needs.

Feedback tells you what to improve.

Part 2 — Collecting and Analyzing Feedback 10–30 min

Methods:

  • Surveys.
  • Interviews.
  • Observation notes.
  • Rating scales.

Analysis:

  • Group similar feedback.
  • Count frequency.
  • Identify patterns.
  • Prioritize: high impact, low effort first.

Part 3 — Analyze Sample Feedback Together 30–55 min

Show 10 pieces of feedback for the library system.

  • Group them.
  • Identify patterns.
  • Prioritize improvements.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Analyze Your Feedback 55–85 min

Analyse your own feedback:

  • Group.
  • Count.
  • Identify patterns.
  • Prioritize 5 improvements.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your analysis.

Discuss: What is your top improvement?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

What is the most important improvement based on your feedback?

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can analyse feedback and prioritize improvements.

Support: Use an analysis template.

Challenge: Use a priority matrix.
