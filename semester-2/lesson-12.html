Energy and Agriculture Automation Systems

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 8 · Automation & Integration Domain-Specific Automation

Why This Matters

Two of the most important domains for automation are energy and agriculture. Smart systems can save water, reduce electricity waste, and help farmers grow more food with less effort. Your project can make a real difference here.

What You Will Learn

  • Apply automation to energy and agriculture.
  • Build a domain-specific automation system.
  • Evaluate the impact of automation.

Words to Know

energy management agriculture automation efficiency sustainability impact

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Automation in Daily Life 0–10 min

Ask yourself: Where is automation used in energy and agriculture?

  • Solar panels.
  • Smart meters.
  • Automatic irrigation.
  • Drones.

Automation improves efficiency and sustainability.

Part 2 — Domain Automation 10–30 min

Energy automation:

  • Monitor usage.
  • Turn off devices when not needed.
  • Optimize solar panel output.

Agriculture automation:

  • Monitor soil moisture.
  • Water only when needed.
  • Detect pests.

Part 3 — Design an Automation Together 30–55 min

Design an energy or agriculture automation for a specific scenario.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Build Your Domain Automation 55–85 min

Build an automation for your domain:

  • Sensors.
  • Decision logic.
  • Actions.
  • Impact evaluation.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your automation.

Discuss: What impact does your automation have?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

Describe one automation and its impact.
