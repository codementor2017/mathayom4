Preparing the Project Portfolio and Demonstration

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 10 · Ethics, Impact & Presentation Portfolio, Demo

Why This Matters

Your project is almost complete. Now you need to tell its story. A portfolio shows everything you built, and a demonstration lets others see your system in action.

What You Will Learn

  • Prepare a project portfolio.
  • Prepare a demonstration.
  • Practise the presentation.

Words to Know

portfolio demonstration documentation showcase rehearsal feedback

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What is a Portfolio? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What should be in a project portfolio?

  • Problem.
  • Design.
  • Prototype.
  • Tests.
  • Feedback.
  • Impact.
  • Reflection.

A portfolio tells the full story of your project.

Part 2 — Portfolio Structure 10–30 min

Follow this structure:

  1. Title and team.
  2. Problem statement.
  3. User needs.
  4. Design and architecture.
  5. Prototype.
  6. Testing and feedback.
  7. Iterations.
  8. Impact assessment.
  9. Reflection.

Demonstration tips:

  • Show the system in action.
  • Explain the technology.
  • Share user feedback.

Part 3 — Prepare the Portfolio Together 30–55 min

Organize your portfolio.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Prepare and Rehearse 55–85 min

Do this:

  • Finalize your portfolio.
  • Prepare a 10-minute presentation.
  • Rehearse.
  • Give feedback to another group.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share rehearsal feedback.

Discuss: What will you improve before the final presentation?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

What is the most important thing to show in your demonstration?
