Usability Testing with Real Users

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 9 · Testing & Iteration User Testing, Feedback

Why This Matters

You may think your system is easy to use — but only real users can tell you for sure. Usability testing is when you watch someone use your system and note where they struggle. It is one of the most valuable things you can do.

What You Will Learn

  • Conduct a usability test.
  • Observe user behaviour.
  • Record usability issues.

Words to Know

usability user testing observation task think-aloud feedback issue

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — What is Usability? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What makes a system easy to use?

  • Simple.
  • Fast.
  • Clear.
  • Forgiving.

Usability testing checks if real users can use your system.

Part 2 — Usability Testing 10–30 min

The process:

  1. Give the user a task.
  2. Watch them use the system.
  3. Ask them to think aloud.
  4. Record issues.
  5. Ask for feedback.

Think-aloud method:

  • "Say what you're thinking as you use it."

Issue categories:

  • Confusing labels.
  • Missing features.
  • Slow response.
  • Errors.

Part 3 — Conduct a Usability Test Together 30–55 min

Conduct a usability test on the library system with a volunteer.

Part 4 — Your Turn: Test with Real Users 55–85 min

Conduct usability tests with at least 2 users.

Record issues and feedback.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Share your usability report.

Discuss: What was the most surprising usability issue?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

List 3 usability issues you found.

Check Your Understanding

You are ready to move on when you can conduct a usability test.

Support: Use a usability test script.

Challenge: Test with users from different backgrounds.
