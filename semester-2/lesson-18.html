Data Privacy and Responsible Innovation

Previous Course Home Next Lesson
100 minutes Phase 10 · Ethics, Impact & Presentation Privacy, Ethics

Why This Matters

Your project collects data from users. That data belongs to them, and you are responsible for protecting it. Data privacy is not just a legal requirement — it is an ethical one. Responsible innovation means building technology that respects people.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify privacy risks in your project.
  • Apply data protection principles.
  • Commit to responsible innovation.

Words to Know

privacy data protection consent anonymization security responsible innovation

Lesson Journey (100 minutes)

Part 1 — Whose Data Do You Use? 0–10 min

Ask yourself: What data does your project collect?

  • Names.
  • Locations.
  • Health data.
  • Usage data.

You are responsible for protecting user data.

Part 2 — Privacy Principles 10–30 min

Follow these principles:

  • Collect only what you need.
  • Ask for consent.
  • Anonymize where possible.
  • Store securely.
  • Delete when no longer needed.
  • Be transparent about how data is used.

Risks to avoid:

  • Data breaches.
  • Unauthorized access.
  • Misuse of data.
  • Privacy violations.

Part 3 — Privacy Audit Together 30–55 min

Audit the library system:

  • What data is collected?
  • Is consent obtained?
  • Is it anonymized?
  • Is it secure?

Part 4 — Your Turn: Privacy Plan 55–85 min

Create a privacy plan for your project:

  • Data collected.
  • Consent process.
  • Security measures.
  • Deletion policy.

Part 5 — Share and Discuss 85–95 min

Present your privacy plan.

Discuss: What is the biggest privacy risk in your project?

Part 6 — Exit Ticket 95–100 min

List 3 privacy principles you will apply.

Check Your Understanding
